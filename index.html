About

I’m a full-stack developer and data engineer. I build the systems people rely on day to day: data pipelines, internal tools, and interfaces that stay clear and fast.

Since 2025 I have worked at South Texas Radiology Group. My title is Data Analyst. The work is data engineering, full-stack development, and systems administration. It runs on Google Cloud, HL7 and FHIR integration, HIPAA-minded handling of clinical data, and Python services in Docker. I am leading the move from a legacy PostgreSQL warehouse onto BigQuery, and I built the pipelines and internal applications the organization now uses. The résumé has the full account.

Before that I built and maintained back-end systems in the legal domain at Linebarger Goggan Blair & Sampson, including fees and fines software shaped by working directly with the attorneys who used it.

In my spare time I am still building Crown’s Call in Unity, and I get outside when I am not coding.
